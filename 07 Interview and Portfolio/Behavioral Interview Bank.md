# Behavioral Interview Bank

Prepare six stories that collectively cover:

1. ambiguous problem and measurable framing;
2. failure you detected and repaired;
3. disagreement and evidence-based decision;
4. security/privacy/safety/accessibility/ethics escalation;
5. constrained delivery and tradeoff;
6. learning a new domain/tool and transferring it to an outcome.

## STAR+E structure

- **Situation:** only context needed to understand stakes.
- **Task:** your responsibility and constraint.
- **Action:** your decisions, alternatives, collaboration, and controls.
- **Result:** evidence and affected outcome; state uncertainty.
- **Evolution:** what you changed afterward and what you would do now.

## Question bank

- Tell me about a time the data contradicted a stakeholder's preferred answer.
- Describe a model/system failure that looked successful at first.
- When did you choose a simpler approach over a fashionable one?
- Tell me about an incident, near miss, or risky launch decision.
- How did you handle a requirement you believed was harmful or unlawful?
- Describe a project with incomplete evaluation evidence.
- When did user feedback reveal a hidden workflow cost?
- Tell me about vendor/tool lock-in or migration pressure.
- How have you supported a teammate or worker doing difficult AI data/review work?
- Describe a time you changed your mind.
- How did you communicate uncertainty to an executive or customer?
- Tell me about an artifact you created that improved team decisions.

## Avoid

Do not make the model the actor, claim credit for a team's work, invent numerical outcomes, reveal confidential information, or present a harmful shortcut as hustle. An honest failed hypothesis with good response often demonstrates more maturity than a flawless story.

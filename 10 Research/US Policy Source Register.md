# U.S. Policy Source Register

Use this as an update checklist, not a substitute for primary text or counsel.

## Current presidential AI landmarks

| Instrument | Date | Operational reading at the cutoff |
|---|---:|---|
| [EO 14179](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/removing-barriers-to-american-leadership-in-artificial-intelligence/) | 2025-01-23 | directed development of the AI Action Plan and revoked EO 14110 |
| [EO 14365](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/12/eliminating-state-law-obstruction-of-national-artificial-intelligence-policy/) | 2025-12-11 | directs executive actions regarding state AI laws and a federal legislative proposal; not itself the requested Act of Congress |
| [EO 14409](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/promoting-advanced-artificial-intelligence-innovation-and-security/) | 2026-06-02 | directs federal AI/cybersecurity work and a voluntary covered-frontier-model framework; does not authorize mandatory model licensing/preclearance |
| [NSPM-11](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/national-security-presidential-memorandum-nspm-11/) | 2026-06-05 | governs AI in the national-security enterprise and replaces NSM-25; not a general private-sector rule |

## Status language

- **Enacted/signed:** became law; still verify effective date and applicability.
- **Final rule:** verify effective/compliance dates and litigation/stays.
- **Proposed rule/draft:** not final.
- **Guidance:** may explain enforcement/expectations but differs from statute/regulation.
- **Executive order/OMB memo:** binds defined executive actors within authority; not automatically a private-sector statute.
- **Framework/standard:** voluntary unless adopted through law, contract, policy, or accreditation.
- **Bill/recommendation:** not law.

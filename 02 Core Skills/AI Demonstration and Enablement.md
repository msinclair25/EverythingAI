# AI Demonstration and Enablement

A trustworthy demonstration teaches capability **and its boundary**. Enablement leaves the audience able to decide and act without dependence on the presenter.

## Demonstration structure

1. audience job and outcome;
2. current baseline;
3. representative input and declared assumptions;
4. live or recorded workflow with source/tool visibility;
5. one intentional failure/uncertainty and recovery path;
6. evaluation result and known limits;
7. architecture, data, security, and cost at audience depth;
8. next test and ownership.

Use synthetic or authorized demo data. Prepare degraded/offline paths. Do not hide retries, hand edits, or cherry-picked cases.

## Enablement design

Set learning outcomes; explain mental models; demonstrate; provide scenario practice or project suggestions; check recall with quiz/flashcards; supply decision guides and escalation; update when product/policy changes. Separate usage training from governance approval.

## Quiz

1. Why demonstrate a failure path? 2. What should be disclosed about a curated demo? 3. Why separate training from authorization? 4. What makes enablement durable?

> [!success]- Answers
> 1. It calibrates trust and shows control/recovery. 2. Selection, retries, edits, data, environment, and limitations. 3. Knowing how to use a tool does not grant permission or establish suitability. 4. Transferable mental models, references, checks, support, and current update ownership.

## Flashcards

Trustworthy demonstration:: A representative capability display that also shows evidence, limitations, and recovery.

Enablement:: Building another person's durable ability to decide and perform with support and boundaries.

Demo contingency:: A prepared safe path for network, model, data, or tool failure.

# Media, Marketing, and Entertainment AI

Add creative direction, brand system, factual review, audience research, advertising/endorsement rules, copyright/license, likeness/voice consent, union/contract requirements, provenance/disclosure, content safety, localization, accessibility, platform rules, and takedown/incident response.

This overlay commonly layers onto [[AI Product Marketing Manager]], [[Generative AI Creative Director]], [[AI Image and Graphic Creator]], [[AI Video Producer]], [[AI Audio and Music Specialist]], and [[AI Trust and Safety Operations Specialist]].

Personalization and targeting add privacy, discrimination, manipulation, and measurement concerns. Synthetic media adds impersonation and election/deepfake concerns. Verify federal and state law, contract, and platform policy for the precise campaign and audience.

## Suggested projects

- Beginner: create a five-asset campaign with a complete human-contribution/provenance log.
- Intermediate: evaluate a creative workflow for consistency, accessibility, brand, rights, and disclosure.
- Advanced: design a consent, content-credential, review, and takedown system for synthetic spokesperson media.
- Advanced GTM: build a claims ledger and launch-readiness packet for an AI feature, separating measured evidence, customer-specific outcomes, and prohibited or unsupported claims.

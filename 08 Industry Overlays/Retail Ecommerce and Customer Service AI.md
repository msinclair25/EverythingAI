# Retail, Ecommerce, and Customer Service AI

Common uses include search/recommendation, merchandising, demand/inventory, pricing, fraud, personalization, support, agent assist, and returns. Add consumer protection, privacy, accessibility, pricing/promotion accuracy, authority for account actions, identity verification, brand/tone, human escalation, and high-volume monitoring.

Common functional owners include [[AI Product Manager]], [[AI Technical Support and User Operations Specialist]], [[AI Customer Success Manager]], [[AI Trust and Safety Operations Specialist]], [[AI Automation Specialist]], and technical delivery roles.

Measure customer effort, resolution quality, repeat contact, refund/error severity, conversion with returns, recommendation diversity, inventory outcome, worker burden, and complaints—not containment or handle time alone.

## Suggested projects

- Beginner: evaluate 50 synthetic support cases for correctness, policy grounding, escalation, and accessibility.
- Intermediate: design a permissioned return/refund assistant with idempotent actions and approval tiers.
- Advanced: compare a recommender by business value, diversity, calibration, user control, and subgroup effects.

Start with [FTC AI materials](https://www.ftc.gov/industry/technology/artificial-intelligence) and current state consumer/privacy rules.

# Education AI

Add learning science, educator workflow, student age/development, accessibility, academic integrity, assessment validity, family/community communication, procurement, data governance, and FERPA/COPPA/state rules as applicable.

Distinguish tutoring, teacher support, administrative systems, admissions/enrollment, assessment/proctoring, research, and student surveillance. Measure learning and equitable access—not engagement or output volume alone. Preserve educator/student agency and alternative paths; test multilingual, disability, socioeconomic, and age-related impacts.

## Suggested projects

- Beginner: design a rubric evaluating whether an AI explanation promotes learning rather than answer copying.
- Intermediate: create an accessible classroom AI use policy with scenarios and source-update plan.
- Advanced: design a randomized or quasi-experimental evaluation of a tutoring feature, including contamination and teacher effects.

Start with [U.S. Department of Education AI resources](https://www.ed.gov/), [FERPA](https://studentprivacy.ed.gov/ferpa), [FTC COPPA](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/rules/childrens-online-privacy-protection-rule-coppa), and current state/district policy.

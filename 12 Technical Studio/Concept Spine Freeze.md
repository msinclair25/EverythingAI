# Concept spine freeze

> [!abstract] Policy
> The builder concept spine is **frozen at 16 notes**. New concept notes are allowed only under the exception rule below. Depth now comes from cases, overlays, syllabus, and exams — not from note 17.

## Frozen set (do not grow)

The ordered list in [[Technical Skills Hub]] is the spine. Do not insert a 17th “also important” concept because a blog post used a new word. Add the word to [[AI Glossary]] and point at an existing note.

Speech and robotics **roles** already exist. They do not automatically unlock new spine notes. They unlock a note only when you are also shipping a case that cannot be taught with [[Vision and Multimodal Systems]], [[The Agent Loop]], or [[The Training Loop]].

## Exception rule

Open a new `type: technical-concept` note only if **all** of these are true:

1. A technical **role** in this vault has no concept that unpacks its core jargon.
2. You are shipping a **case** in the same change that uses that jargon under a contract.
3. The term cannot be a glossary row plus a paragraph on an existing note.
4. The note will follow the six-part template (plain idea, why, jargon, teaching numbers, quiz, transfer).

If any item is false, patch an existing note or the glossary.

## Why freeze

Sixteen notes already cover the terms that show up in AI/ML/LLM design reviews: tokens, leakage, metrics, KV, context, contracts, retrieval, adaptation, embeddings, training, agents, injection, decoding, calibration, quantization, vision. More notes without a syllabus make the vault harder to *use*.

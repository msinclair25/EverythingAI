## A posting-mapping worksheet

1. **Outcome:** What changes if this person succeeds?
2. **Artifacts:** What do they repeatedly produce?
3. **Decisions:** What can they decide without approval?
4. **Interfaces:** Who consumes their work?
5. **Failure ownership:** Who responds when the system is wrong, slow, insecure, costly, or harmful?
6. **Evidence:** Code, research, product metrics, controls, creative work, operations quality, or customer outcomes?

The answers matter more than title similarity.

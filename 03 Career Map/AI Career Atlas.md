# AI Career Atlas

The atlas organizes AI work by the outcome a person owns. It includes established occupations, AI specializations of established occupations, and emerging titles. See [[Coverage, Evidence, and Limits]] before interpreting the list as a census.

Open [[AI Career Landscape.canvas|the interactive AI career landscape]] to pan, zoom, and follow its linked role cards.

## 1 · Research and applied science

- [[AI ML Research Scientist]] — creates and validates new methods or scientific knowledge.
- [[Research Engineer]] — turns research ideas into reliable experimental systems.
- [[Applied Scientist]] — applies experimental and modeling depth to product or business problems.
- [[AI Safety and Alignment Researcher]] — studies and reduces harmful or misaligned model behavior.

## 2 · Model and application engineering

- [[ML Engineer]] — builds, integrates, and operates learned systems.
- [[AI Engineer]] — ships applications using foundation models and AI services.
- [[LLM Engineer]] — specializes in language-model application behavior and adaptation.
- [[Agentic AI Engineer]] — builds systems that plan, use tools, maintain state, and act.
- [[NLP Engineer]] — develops systems for language understanding and generation.
- [[Computer Vision Engineer]] — develops systems that interpret or generate visual data.
- [[Speech AI Engineer]] — works on speech recognition, synthesis, audio understanding, and voice systems.
- [[Robotics and Embodied AI Engineer]] — connects perception, planning, learning, and control in physical systems.

## 3 · Data, retrieval, and knowledge

- [[Data Scientist - AI ML]] — uses data, experiments, and models to answer decisions and build predictive solutions.
- [[ML Data Engineer]] — supplies reliable, governed, model-ready data and features.
- [[RAG and Knowledge Engineer]] — builds retrieval and grounding systems around organizational knowledge.
- [[Synthetic Data Engineer]] — designs and validates generated data for training, testing, privacy, or simulation.
- [[Taxonomist and Ontology Specialist]] — defines controlled concepts and relationships.
- [[Knowledge Manager]] — makes organizational knowledge findable, governed, and reusable.

## 4 · Platform, operations, performance, and hardware

- [[MLOps Engineer]] — automates and governs model delivery and operations.
- [[AI Platform Engineer]] — builds shared infrastructure and paved roads for AI teams.
- [[Inference and Performance Engineer]] — optimizes serving speed, throughput, memory, reliability, and cost.
- [[AI Hardware and Accelerator Engineer]] — designs or optimizes compute systems for AI workloads.

## 5 · Evaluation, security, safety, and assurance

- [[AI Evaluation Engineer]] — designs measurement systems and diagnoses behavior.
- [[AI Security Engineer]] — secures AI components, data flows, and integrations.
- [[AI Red Teamer]] — adversarially tests models and applications with authorization.
- [[AI Trust and Safety Operations Specialist]] — investigates platform abuse, enforces safety policy, and improves operational controls.
- [[Responsible AI Specialist]] — operationalizes trustworthy and responsible practices across the lifecycle.
- [[Model Risk Manager]] — independently challenges and controls model risk, often in financial services.
- [[AI Auditor and Assurance Specialist]] — evaluates evidence against criteria and reports findings.
- [[AI Ethics Researcher]] — analyzes normative questions, impacts, and decision frameworks.

## 6 · Product, program, strategy, and transformation

- [[AI Product Manager]]
- [[Technical AI Product Manager]]
- [[AI Program Manager]]
- [[AI Business Analyst]]
- [[AI Strategy Consultant]]
- [[AI Transformation Lead]]
- [[Chief AI Officer and Head of AI]] — owns enterprise AI direction, operating model, accountability, and portfolio outcomes; this is an executive destination, not an entry-level track.
- [[AI Automation Specialist]]

# How an AI assistant should use this vault

Read this before editing Anything AI / EverythingAI. The human owner’s contract beats this page if they conflict.

## What this vault is

A US-focused **career map + taught jargon + worked decisions**. It is not a lab school, not legal advice, and not a live model leaderboard.

## Do

1. Open [[Everything AI - Home]], [[Coverage, Evidence, and Limits]], and [[Concept Spine Freeze]] first.
2. Prefer **patching** an existing note over creating a 17th concept. Spine is frozen at 16.
3. New teaching notes use the existing templates: concept (plain idea → why → jargon → teaching numbers → quiz → transfer) or case (Northline teaching envelope, labeled constructed).
4. Keep `as_of` / `last_verified` honest. Do not invent statutes or prices.
5. After material adds, update [[Vault Changelog]], Home counts (by counting files, not guessing), and the relevant hub.
6. Wikilink related roles and concepts. A wikilink must match a note filename or its `title:` field.
7. Propose vault updates when you finish a task. Do not leave orphan notes.

## Do not

- Do not add prescriptive labs or copy-paste tutorial tracks.
- Do not cite teaching numbers as measured results.
- Do not thicken all ten industry overlays because three were thickened. Exception: a user asks for a specific sector.
- Do not expand the 47 nontechnical roles unless asked.
- Do not commit secrets, PHI, or live customer data into examples.
- Do not treat EOs or OMB memos as statutes.
